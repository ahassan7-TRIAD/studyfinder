Study Finder

Support

Study Finder is an iPhone app that lets clinicians browse the research studies currently enrolling, check eligibility criteria and contact each study's coordinator.

Contact us

For help with the app, coordinator access or a problem with a study listing, email:

studyfinder@hvi.edu

We aim to reply within two business days.

Frequently asked questions

Clinician or study coordinator: which should I choose?

Choose Clinician to browse studies, see inclusion and exclusion criteria, and view or print consent and HIPAA forms. Choose Study coordinator only if you manage studies; coordinators can add, edit and remove studies and their forms. You can switch at any time from the role button at the top right of the study list.

How do I get the coordinator passcode?

The passcode is shared only with study coordinators on the team. Ask your team lead or email us from your work address.

I don't see a study I expected

Pull down on the study list to refresh it. Check that no filters are on (the filter button is filled in when they are) and that the search box is empty. If the study is still missing, contact its coordinator or email us.

The app says it couldn't load studies

Check your internet connection and tap Try again. Some VPNs are blocked by the service the app uses to store studies; if you use a VPN, try again with it turned off.

How do I print a consent or HIPAA form?

Open the study, find the form under Consent forms or HIPAA forms, and tap the printer button. You can print to any AirPrint printer or save the form as a PDF.

Does the app collect information about me or my patients?

No. Don't enter patient information into the app. See the privacy policy.
